Create your own board and explore how rook placements affect the rook polynomial.

Step 1: Choose Board Size

Step 2: Build & Explore Your Board

Restrict squares to create a custom board, or place rooks to experiment with non-attacking arrangements.

Note: The rooks you place are used to check a particular arrangement. They do not change the rook polynomial.

Current mode: Place Rook

Step 3: Calculate Rook Polynomial

The rook polynomial describes all possible non-attacking rook placements on the available squares of your board.

The calculation considers every possible number of rooks, from 0 up to the size of the board.
